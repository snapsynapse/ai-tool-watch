## Pricing

| Plan | Price | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| Free | $0 | Everyday Meta AI use; daily limits on media generation |
| Meta One Core | $7.99/mo | More image and video generation, plus Instagram Plus, WhatsApp Plus and Facebook Plus features |
| Meta One Premium | $19.99/mo | Everything in Core with the largest Meta AI media-generation allowance |

Meta One also sells single-app plans (WhatsApp Plus $2.99/mo, Instagram Plus and Facebook Plus $3.99/mo) and creator and business bundles from $14.99/mo. None adds Meta AI usage, so they aren't listed. Meta's 2026-09-15 announcement says plans are available globally; Meta's help center still says Meta One is in limited testing and not available everywhere, that Premium is bought on iPhone or Android, and that pricing and availability vary by region.
